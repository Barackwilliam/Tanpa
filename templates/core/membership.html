How to join

Four steps to an active membership

Create an account

Register with your email, professional details and a passport photo.

Pay your fees

Pay the entry fee and the annual subscription by mobile money or bank.

Get your number

Once your payment is confirmed you receive a permanent TANPA membership number.

Download your ID

Print your ID card. Renew every year to stay active.

Who is an active member?

Active

Entry fee paid and the annual subscription for the current year paid.

Non-active

A registered member who has not yet paid this year's subscription.

Membership benefits

  • Official TANPA membership number and ID card
  • Reduced fees for the annual scientific conference & AGM
  • Continuous professional development (CPD) opportunities
  • A voice in national anaesthesia policy and advocacy
  • Networking with anaesthesia providers across all regions
  • Voting rights at the Annual General Meeting
